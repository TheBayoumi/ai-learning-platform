# V00 Candidate Role Evidence

**Evidence cutoff:** 2026-07-10
**Roadmap decision:** `Revise`
**Gate status:** Not passed
**Next phase unlocked:** No

This is a product-validation evidence dossier, not a `RoleProfile`, curriculum, assessment, simulation Blueprint, or readiness claim. It does not select a role. It compares the provisional Target with credible alternatives and records what is still required to finish `V00`.

## Comparison Method

Every candidate is evaluated on the same dimensions:

1. exact Target tuple;
2. current demand in the stated labor market and seniority;
3. stability of a bounded common role baseline;
4. objective assessment feasibility;
5. realistic simulation feasibility;
6. feasibility of no-hint, reasoning, retention, transfer, unique-work, and blinded-review evidence;
7. access to two qualified practitioners;
8. access to a real channel able to reach 20-50 eligible adults; and
9. expected infrastructure and evaluation cost.

Evidence labels are enforceable:

- **Supported:** a direct source supports the bounded statement it accompanies.
- **Directional:** the source indicates plausibility but does not match the exact role, seniority, labor market, or freshness needed by the gate.
- **Assumption:** a comparison definition or estimate that has not been approved as product scope.
- **Unverified:** required evidence is absent.

The gate is conjunctive. A plausible assessment or simulation cannot compensate for absent demand, practitioners, recruitment access, or cost acceptance.

### C1 - Junior Python Backend Engineer

- **Role:** Junior Python Backend Engineer; whether Python belongs in the role name or only the Stack Overlay remains under test.
- **Seniority:** Entry-level or junior individual contributor.
- **Labor market:** Egypt and MENA-based adults targeting Egypt-local, MENA-regional, or English-speaking remote roles. These submarkets must be measured separately and may not be pooled into one demand or baseline claim.
- **Learner baseline:** Adults aged 18+ with basic Python syntax and Git but without production backend competence or defensible work evidence.
- **Timeline:** 4-6 months for broader preparation; this is the constitutional planning assumption, not an outcome guarantee.
- **Overlays:** Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, REST APIs, Git, automated testing, Docker, basic CI, debugging, documentation, and engineering communication. No Industry or Company Overlay.

It appears feasible to vary requirements, data, defects, and constraints per learner while retaining shared blueprints and rubrics. This is a feasibility hypothesis only.

## Learning-Evidence Feasibility

The four candidates can plausibly expose the constitutional evidence types, but no item, rubric, Blueprint, evaluator, or instrument has been validated.

| Required evidence | Feasibility test for any advancing candidate |
| --- | --- |
| Independent no-hint performance | The learner completes a bounded task while the tutor is silent and assistance is recorded as none. |
| Reasoning | The learner explains choices, evidence, trade-offs, and rejected alternatives without receiving the answer. |
| Delayed retention | A materially equivalent but unseen task is performed after the constitutional 7- and 30-day windows. |
| Transfer | Near-transfer changes context or constraints; applicable far-transfer changes the problem framing without reusing the taught answer. |
| Unique work | A validated shared Blueprint produces a distinct Instance with recorded seed, parameters, dependencies, and assistance policy. |
| Anti-dependency | The learner must modify, debug, or defend work after generation; accepted evidence cannot depend on tutor completion. |
| Blinded review | Qualified practitioners score de-identified artifacts using a validated shared rubric without seeing system predictions. |

This table tests whether future validation is possible. It does not establish validity or authorize asset production.

## Recommendation and Decision

**Decision: `Revise`.**

No candidate receives an evidence ranking from this dossier. Junior Python Backend Engineer remains the inherited provisional candidate only because the constitution names it; this is not support for selection and does not protect it from replacement. Junior Data Analyst, Junior Software Tester, and SOC Analyst L1 remain comparator hypotheses. The SOC candidate carries an explicit, unverified risk that realistic tooling, isolation, industry variation, and evaluation cost may be less favorable.

The evidence does not yet justify `Narrow`. One remote backend sample requests more experience and autonomy than local samples, so Egypt-local and English-remote markets must be measured separately; one sample cannot establish that the broader market is incoherent. The evidence also cannot justify `Continue`, because every external-access condition and several feasibility conditions remain unmet.

### Smallest unblocking sequence

First, preregister one common sampling window, source policy, market boundary, and per-candidate sampling rule, then collect a symmetric, deduplicated sample for all four candidates. No candidate may be ranked from the uneven sample in this dossier.

For the resulting finalist, obtain privacy-safe written availability confirmation from two currently practicing professionals and written confirmation from one channel owner that the channel can reach 20-50 adults matching the learner baseline. Store personal details and correspondence outside Git; record only pseudonymous evidence identifiers and controlled references here. Also document the practitioner qualification rule and expected-cost acceptance boundary. Until the rerun passes every conjunctive condition:

- the provisional Target remains replaceable;
- no `RoleProfile`, proof slice, curriculum, assessment, rubric, or simulation may be produced;
- `V01` remains ineligible; and
- no product or readiness claim is authorized.
