## Scope

1. Define one symmetric comparison method.
2. Compare the provisional candidate with Junior Data Analyst, Junior Software Tester, and SOC Analyst L1.
3. Give each candidate a complete, explicitly provisional Target tuple.
4. Assess current demand signals, common-baseline stability, objective assessment feasibility, realistic simulation feasibility, learning-evidence feasibility, practitioner access, learner-channel access, and relative expected cost.
5. Separate facts, directional signals, assumptions, and unknowns.
6. Record the smallest evidence-acquisition action needed to rerun the gate.
7. Issue one gate decision without advancing to `V01`.

## Domain and API Contracts

This phase introduces no runtime domain or API contract. Terms such as Target, Stack Overlay, `RoleProfile`, Blueprint, and Instance retain their constitutional definitions. Candidate tuples in the dossier are research definitions only and must not be imported as platform state.

## Data and Migration Implications

There is no persistent schema and no migration. The dossier stores only public-source metadata, paraphrased findings, assumptions, and gate status. Any future private practitioner or recruitment proof must remain in controlled storage; Git may contain only a pseudonymous evidence identifier, qualification summary, date, status, and controlled reference.

## Failure Handling

- A missing, stale, inaccessible, or ambiguous source is recorded as missing evidence, not converted into support.
- Aggregate occupation data is not treated as role-, seniority-, stack-, or labor-market-specific demand.
- A job posting proves only that one described role existed at the observed source; it does not prove market volume or a stable baseline.
- Practitioner availability is not practitioner approval.
- Hypothetical audience reach is not a recruitment channel.
- Qualitative cost comparison is not an approved ceiling or measured cost baseline.
- Any missing mandatory gate returns `Revise`, `Narrow`, or `Stop`, never assumed success.

## Privacy and Security Implications

Only public occupational sources are cited. No applicant, reviewer, practitioner, or learner personal data is collected. External confirmations needed later must use access-controlled records and privacy-safe references. No raw correspondence is committed.

## Observability Requirements

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The provisional candidate and at least two credible alternatives use complete Target tuples and one comparison method.
2. Demand evidence distinguishes broad occupation signals from current, role-specific evidence.
3. Assessment and simulation analysis covers no-hint performance, reasoning, delayed retention, unseen transfer, unique work, and blinded practitioner review as feasibility only.
4. Missing practitioner, recruitment, and cost proof is explicit.
5. The recommendation includes rejection or hold reasons for every candidate.
6. The phase ends in exactly one roadmap decision and does not unlock `V01` without a passed gate.
7. Independent review finds no uncorrected blocking or high-severity defect.

## Evidence Gate

`Continue` requires one candidate to have all of the following: sufficient current demand in the exact labor market and seniority; a stable bounded common baseline; an objectively assessable meaningful slice; a realistic simulation path; acceptable expected infrastructure and evaluation cost; confirmed availability of at least two qualified practitioners; and a confirmed real channel able to reach 20-50 suitable adults. These conditions are conjunctive.

Before a rerun can claim `Continue`, the team must preregister a current-demand sampling window and sufficiency rule, practitioner qualification rule, recruitment proof format, and expected-cost acceptance boundary. This plan does not invent those approvals.

## Rollback Strategy

No runtime, schema, dependency, or constitutional state changes exist. ## Decision Rule

- **Continue:** every conjunctive gate has direct evidence.
- **Revise:** the comparison method or evidence is incomplete or weak, while at least one candidate remains plausible.
- **Narrow:** evidence supports a coherent smaller role or labor market but not the broader tuple.
- **Stop:** no candidate has a credible path through the gate.

The evidence dossier confirms `Revise`: public sources support candidate feasibility hypotheses, but the repository still contains no practitioner confirmation, recruitment-channel proof, approved demand sufficiency rule, or cost-acceptance boundary. `V01` remains locked.
